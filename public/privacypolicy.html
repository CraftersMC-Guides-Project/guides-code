Official Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026 • Applies to the website, Feather Bot, and community services

Sections 1–8

lock_outline 1. Information We Do Not Collect

CraftersMC Guides is designed as a public, static information portal. We believe that privacy should be the default:

  • check_circle No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or passwords.
  • check_circle No Browsing Trackers: We do not track your activity across other websites or deploy third-party advertising tracking pixels.
  • check_circle No Mandatory Registration: All guides, tools, calculators, and bazaar data are publicly accessible without an account.

cookie 2. Cookies & Browser Local Storage

Our website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. We use browser localStorage strictly for functional client-side features:

  • check_circle Theme Preferences: Storing whether your device prefers Light Mode or Dark Mode (darkMode key).
  • check_circle HTML Partial Caching: Caching the header navbar and sidebar templates so pages load instantaneously as you navigate.
info

This data is stored purely on your local device and is never transmitted to our servers or shared with any third party.

link 3. Third-Party Integrations & Public Endpoints

To provide live SkyBlock market and player exploration features, the site occasionally communicates with public third-party services:

  • check_circle Public Game APIs: Market orders and player statistics are fetched from public CraftersMC or server endpoints.
  • check_circle Avatar & Skin Rendering: Character heads and skin models are displayed via public skin proxies (e.g. mc-heads.net).
  • check_circle External Links: Links to external platforms (such as Discord, GitHub, or YouTube) are governed by the privacy policies of those respective services.

smart_toy 4. CraftersMC AI & Feather Bot

When interacting with automated tooling, privacy safeguards remain strictly enforced:

  • check_circle Ephemeral Prompts: AI queries processed through Cloudflare Workers AI are anonymized, processed in memory, and never stored permanently.
  • check_circle No Model Training on User Data: Your queries are never used to train or fine-tune public language models.
  • check_circle Discord Bot Data: Feather Bot only reads commands issued directly to it via slash commands (/). It does not log server chat messages.

shield 5. Security & Infrastructure

CraftersMC Guides is deployed globally across Cloudflare's serverless edge infrastructure. This guarantees:

  • check_circle Strict HTTPS enforcement with modern TLS encryption protocols.
  • check_circle Protection against Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks and malicious traffic.

family_restroom 6. Children's Privacy

Minecraft and SkyBlock are played by players of all ages. Because we do not collect personal information of any kind, our website is fully safe for players of any age, including children under 13.

history_edu 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our website features, bot capabilities, or legal requirements. When updates occur, the Last Updated date at the top will be updated accordingly. Continued use of the site signifies acceptance of any updates.

contact_support 8. Contact & Privacy Questions

If you have questions, concerns, or feedback regarding our privacy practices, our team is always accessible:

  • check_circle Discord: Join our Official Discord Server and open a support ticket.
  • check_circle Email: Reach us directly at craftersmcguides@gmail.com.
