Applications FAQ

Answers to common questions regarding future recruitment and openings:

FAQ

help_outline When will new posts or roles open?

Openings occur whenever the project undertakes major expansions, such as new SkyBlock island updates, new tool developments, or expanded Feather Bot features. Openings are always announced first on Discord.

help_outline Can I apply early before an opening is posted?

We do not accept unsolicited applications while posts are closed. The best way to get noticed is being an active, helpful community member and contributing feedback or pull requests.

help_outline Is this an official CraftersMC server staff role?

No. CraftersMC Guides is an independent community project run by dedicated players. Contributing here does not give you in-game moderator or administrative powers on the server.

help_outline Where will openings be announced?

When a position opens, it will be posted in the #announcements channel of our Discord server and updated immediately on this page.
