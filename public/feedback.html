How to Submit Helpful Feedback

Follow these tips to help us investigate and implement your feedback quickly:

4 Tips
01

Specify the Page

Mention the exact page URL, guide chapter, or calculator where you noticed the issue.

02

Attach Screenshots

In-game screenshots of updated recipes, prices, or NPC interfaces speed up corrections tremendously.

03

Explain the Expected Result

Tell us what happened versus what you expected to see (e.g. correct formula vs current calculation).

04

Get Credited

Helpful bug finders and data contributors are recognized in our team and contributor records!

Feedback FAQ

Common questions about submitting feedback and suggestions:

Questions

help_outline Why did you stop using web form submissions?

Web form submissions made it difficult to follow up with players, ask clarifying questions, or notify you when your issue was fixed. Discord allows instant, collaborative two-way conversation.

help_outline How fast does the team review feedback?

Active team members and developers monitor Discord channels every day. Most typos and simple inaccuracies are verified and patched within 24 to 48 hours.

help_outline Can I suggest ideas for Feather Bot?

Yes! Feature suggestions for Feather Bot (such as new slash commands, alert triggers, or price tracking options) are welcomed in #suggestions.

help_outline Can I submit code changes directly?

Yes! If you are a developer, our front-end codebase is hosted on GitHub. Feel free to open a pull request with your bug fix or UI improvement.
