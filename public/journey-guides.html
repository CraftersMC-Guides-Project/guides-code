4 Golden Rules for Fast Progression

Keep these core principles in mind to maximize your efficiency and coin generation:

Best Practices
Rule 01 schedule

Automate With Minions

Minions work while you are offline. Prioritize unlocking minion slots early to generate millions of coins and passive collection exp every day.

Rule 02 toll

Accessories = Free Stats

A legendary weapon is weak without talismans. Collect every talisman you can afford; the passive magical power scales all your damage.

Rule 03 storefront

Never Instant Sell

Instant selling to the Bazaar or NPCs throws away 10%–30% in profits. Always place Sell Offers to maximize coin returns on materials.

Rule 04 event

Track Live Events

Spooky Festivals, Jerry events, and Traveling Zoo visits yield exclusive items and heavy market profits. Use our SkyCalendar tool to never miss one.
